II.2.4.
Description of the procurement
With this publication, Stromnetz Berlin GmbH intends to call on applicants to tender for the supply of modern metering devices according to the Measuring Station Operation Law (Messstellenbetriebsgesetz) dated 2 September 2016, hereinafter referred to as ‘mMe’. Detailed information is available in the participation documents and the technical descriptions made available by the issuing agency. Applicant's questions will be submitted to the issuing agency via the question and answer forum placed in the electronic tender assistant (see below).
This contract notice allows the option of an invitation to tender at a later date. Furthermore, Stromnetz Berlin GmbH intends to contract only those suppliers for prospective mMe delivery orders who have previously qualified in the procedure for this verification system.
You will find further information in:
https://root.deutsche-evergabe.de/portal/

Precise information on deadline(s) for review procedures:
In accordance with Section 160 (3) clause 1 Nos 1 to 4 of the GWB, requests for a review are not permitted

d) where more than 15 calendar days have elapsed after receipt of notification from the contracting entity that they do not intend to remedy a complaint (Section 160 (3) clause 1 No. 4 GWB).
